Cara pakai

EASY — semua blok tersedia (properti, event, aksi).
HARDMODE — blok cuma case; isi coding pakai DSL (syntax sederhana) yang dikompilasi jadi JS mod BunnyLoader.

Format case (HARDMODE)

item NovaBlade:
  damage 42
  size 40 40
  rare orange
  sound 1
  recipe: goldbar x12 @ anvil
  on use:
    dash 11
    say "Dashing!"

npc DummyKing:
  life 500
  damage 30
  defense 12
  size 48
  boss
  aistyle walker
  on ai:
    dust 3 6 speed 2
    light 0.8 0.2 0.2
  on death:
    drop "NovaBlade" 1 1

proj NovaShot:
  damage 25
  speed 14
  pierce 2
  aistyle straight
  on ai:
    dust 1 15 speed 1

tile NovaOre:
  solid
  hardness 65
  mapcolor 55 230 165

Case types

item · npc · proj · tile · world — satu case = satu class mod.

Hasil export

File .bl (zip) berisi manifest.json (blVersion 2, entry main.js), kode per-object di content/Code/…, texture placeholder otomatis, icon, description & license. Tinggal taruh di folder mods BunnyLoader.
